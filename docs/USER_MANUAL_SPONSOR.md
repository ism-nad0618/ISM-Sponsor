## Role Overview

### Sponsor Responsibilities

As a **Sponsor**, you have access to a self-service portal where you can manage your sponsorship information and monitor your sponsored students.

**What You Can Do:**
- ✅ View your sponsor profile information
- ✅ View all students you sponsor
- ✅ View all your Letters of Guarantee (LoGs)
- ✅ Track LoG status and coverage details
- ✅ Submit change requests for your contact information
- ✅ Generate reports about your sponsorships
- ✅ Download LoG documents
- ✅ View billing summaries

**What You Cannot Do:**
- ❌ Create or edit sponsorrecords (contact Admissions)
- ❌ Create or edit LoGs (managed by Admissions)
- ❌ View other sponsors' information
- ❌ Approve change requests (Administrator approval)
- ❌ Access system administration functions

**Your Typical Workflow:**
1. Log into your sponsor management
2. Review dashboard for updates
3. Check active LoGs for your students
4. Verify coverage information
5. Submit change requests if contact info changes
6. Generate reports as needed
7. Download LoG documents for your records

### Key Concepts

**Sponsor Profile:**
- Your organization information
- Contact details for school communication
- Billing information
- Status (Active/Inactive)

**Students:**
- Individuals you sponsor for education
- Each student has unique Student ID
- Sponsors can support multiple students
- Student information maintained by school

**Letters of Guarantee (LoGs):**
- Formal documents defining your coverage commitments
- One LoG per student per school year
- Status: Draft → Active → Past
- Contains coverage rules (what you cover, percentages, limits)
- Legally binding financial commitments

**Coverage Rules:**
- Define what expenses you cover
- Categories: Medical, Dental, Tuition, Books, etc.
- Coverage levels: Full (100%), Partial (split %), or None
- Special conditions or annual limits

**Change Requests:**
- Required for updating your contact information
- Submitted for Administrator approval
- Tracks justification and supporting documents
- Status: Pending → Approved/Rejected

**School Years:**
- Academic years (e.g., 2025-2026)
- Controls which students and LoGs you see
- 

Always verify correct school year selected

### Understanding Coverage Rules

**Coverage Levels:**

1. **100% - Fully Covered:**
   - You pay 100% of the expense
   - Parent/guardian pays nothing
   - School bills you directly

2. **Split Coverage (e.g., 50%):**
   - You pay specified percentage
   - Parent/guardian pays remainder
   - Clearly defined in LoG

3. **0% - Not Covered:**
   - You pay nothing
   - Parent/guardian responsible
   - For reference only in LoG

**Special Conditions:**
- Annual limits (maximums per year)
- Per-incident limits
- Pre-authorization requirements
- Excluded items or services
- Documentation requirements

**Important Notes:**
- Coverage rules are binding commitments
- Changes require new LoG or amendment
- School follows LoG strictly for billing
- Always review carefully before acceptance
- Contact Admissions for clarifications

## Common Workflows

### Scenario 1: New School Year - Verify LoGs

**Situation:** New school year starting, verify all students have active LoGs

**Steps:**

1. Log into Sponsor Management
2. Check Dashboard statistics
   - "Active LoGs" should match "Active Students"
   - If numbers don't match, investigation needed

3. Navigate to "My LoGs"
4. Filter by current school year (e.g., 2025-2026)
5. Verify each student has an Active LoG
6. If any student missing LoG:
   - Check if LoG is in Draft status
   - Contact Admissions: admissions@ism.edu.ph
   - Provide student name and ID

7. Download all active LoGs for your records
8. Review coverage details for any changes

**Timeline:** Complete by first week of school year

---

### Scenario 2: Office Relocation - Update Address

**Situation:** Your organization is moving to a new office

**Steps:**

1. Gather required documents:
   - New lease agreement or proof of address
   - Company memo announcing relocation
   - Utility bill with new address (if available)

2. Log into Sponsor Management
3. Navigate to: Change Requests → Submit New Request
4. Select "Update Address"
5. Enter new address details carefully
6. Provide justification:
   ```
   "Company relocating to new headquarters.
   Effective Date: [Date]
   Please update all correspondence to new address."
   ```

7. Upload supporting documents
8. Submit request
9. Note Request ID for tracking

10. Wait for Administrator review (typically 1-2 business days)
11. Check email for approval notification
12. Verify your profile shows updated address

**Pro Tip:** Submit request 2-3 weeks before move to ensure timely processing.

---

### Scenario 3: Verify Student Coverage Before Medical Visit

**Situation:** Sponsored student needs medical attention, verify coverage

**Steps:**

1. Log into Sponsor Management
2. Navigate to "My Students"
3. Search for student by name or ID
4. Click student name to view details
5. Verify LoG Status is "Active"
6. Review coverage rules:
   - Check "Medical Expenses" is covered
   - Note any special conditions
   - Check for pre-authorization requirements

7. If Medical is 100% covered:
   - Proceed with medical visit
   - School will bill you directly
   - Ensure hospital/clinic uses LoG ID

8. If pre-authorization required:
   - Contact Admissions before visit
   - Provide LoG ID and treatment details
   - Wait for authorization

9. Download LoG document
10. Provide copy to medical facility if requested

**Important:** Always verify coverage BEFORE treatment when possible.

---

### Scenario 4: End of School Year - Review & Archive

**Situation:** School year ending, review sponsorships and prepare for next year

**Steps:**

1. **Review Current Year:**
   - Log into portal
   - Navigate to "My LoGs"
   - Select current school year
   - Generate "My LoGs Report"
   - Save report for your records

2. **Verify All Billings:**
   - Check that all expected charges received
   - Cross-reference with coverage rules
   - Note any discrepancies
   - Contact Cashier if questions: cashier@ism.edu.ph

3. **Archive Documents:**
   - Download all LoG documents (PDF)
   - Download year-end report
   - Save to your financial records
   - Keep for 7 years (tax purposes)

4. **Prepare for Next Year:**
   - Confirm which students continuing
   - Check for address/contact changes
   - Update information if needed (submit change requests early)
   - Anticipate new LoGs for next year

5. **Communication:**
   - Respond to any Admissions requests promptly
   - Confirm sponsorship intentions for next year
   - Update billing contact if changed

**Timeline:** Complete by end of May (before summer break)

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues & Solutions

#### Issue 1: Cannot Login

**Symptoms:**
- "Invalid username or password" error
- Login page keeps reloading
- "Account locked" message

**Solutions:**

1. **Verify Credentials:**
   - Check username (case-sensitive)
   - Verify password (check Caps Lock)
   - Try copy-paste from password manager

2. **Reset Password:**
   - Click "Forgot Password" on login page
   - Enter your username
   - Check email for reset link
   - Follow instructions

3. **Account Locked:**
   - Too many failed login attempts
   - Wait 30 minutes and try again
   - Or contact IT: support@ism.edu.ph

4. **Browser Issues:**
   - Clear browser cache/cookies
   - Try different browser
   - Disable browser extensions
   - Try incognito/private mode

#### Issue 2: Dashboard Shows No Data

**Symptoms:**
- "0 Active Students" displayed
- "0 Active LoGs" shown
- Empty lists everywhere

**Solutions:**

1. **Verify School Year:**
   - Check school year selector
   - Make sure correct year selected
   - Try "All Years" to see historical data

2. **Check Sponsor ID:**
   - Verify you're logged in with correct account
   - Check dashboard shows your organization name
   - If wrong account, logout and re-login

3. **Data Not Yet Loaded:**
   - If new sponsor, data may not be entered yet
   - Contact Admissions to verify your account setup
   - Allow 1-2 business days after registration

4. **Technical Issue:**
   - Refresh page (F5 or Ctrl+R)
   - Clear cache and reload
   - Try different device
   - Contact support if persists

#### Issue 3: Cannot Submit Change Request

**Symptoms:**
- "Submit" button disabled/greyed out
- Error message when submitting
- Form won't validate

**Solutions:**

1. **Required Fields:**
   - Ensure all fields marked with * are filled
   - Justification must be at least 20 characters
   - Proposed information must be different from current

2. **File Upload Issues:**
   - Check file size (max 5MB)
   - Use supported formats (PDF, JPG, PNG, DOCX)
   - Try uploading smaller file
   - Files optional (can submit without)

3. **Browser Issues:**
   - Try different browser
   - Disable ad blockers
   - Enable JavaScript
   - Clear cache

4. **Pending Request:**
   - Only one request of same type allowed at a time
   - Check "My Change Requests" for pending items
   - Wait for approval/rejection before submitting new one

#### Issue 4: LoG Document Won't Download

**Symptoms:**
- Click "Download" does nothing
- PDF opens but is blank
- Download fails/errors

**Solutions:**

1. **Browser Settings:**
   - Allow pop-ups from site
   - Check download folder permissions
   - Disable PDF browser viewer, use Adobe Reader

2. **File Generation:**
   - Wait 5-10 seconds after clicking
   - Large LoGs take time to generate
   - Check browser's download manager

3. **PDF Reader:**
   - Install/update Adobe Reader
   - Try opening in different PDF viewer
   - Right-click → "Save link as"

4. **Alternative:**
   - Request email copy from Admissions
   - Try printing (Ctrl+P) instead of downloading
   - View LoG details on screen, screenshot if needed

#### Issue 5: Wrong Information Displayed

**Symptoms:**
- Student list shows wrong students
- Coverage details incorrect
- Profile shows old information

**Solutions:**

1. **Cache Issue:**
   - Hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
   - Clear browser cache completely
   - Logout and login again

2. **Pending Changes:**
   - If you submitted change request, it's not applied until approved
   - Check "My Change Requests" for status
   - Old information displays until approval

3. **Data Entry Error:**
   - If information is actually wrong in system:
   - Contact Admissions: admissions@ism.edu.ph
   - Provide correct information
   - Reference specific LoG ID or Student ID

4. **Wrong Account:**
   - Verify you're logged in as correct sponsor
   - Check dashboard header shows your organization
   - Logout and login with correct credentials

## FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: How do I get my initial login credentials?**

A: Your credentials are provided by ISM Admissions when you register as a sponsor. You'll receive an email with your username and temporary password. Change the password on first login.

---

**Q: Can I sponsor students from multiple schools?**

A: This system is specific to ISM (International School Manila). If you sponsor students at other schools, they would have separate systems.

---

**Q: How long does it take for a change request to be approved?**

A: Typically 1-2 business days. Complex requests requiring verification may take up to 5 business days. You'll receive email notification when decided.

---

**Q: Can I cancel a submitted change request?**

A: Yes, if it's still in "Pending" status. Go to "My Change Requests", click the request, and click "Cancel Request". Once approved or rejected, it cannot be cancelled.

---

### LoG Questions

**Q: What happens if a LoG is in "Draft" status?**

A: Draft LoGs are not yet active. Coverage does not apply until Administrator activates the LoG. Contact Admissions if a LoG remains in Draft status for more than 5 business days.

---

**Q: Can I edit an active LoG?**

A: No. Active LoGs are binding agreements and cannot be edited directly. If changes are needed, Admissions must create a new LoG or amendment. Significant changes may require Administrator approval.

---

**Q: What does "Split Coverage" mean?**

A: Split coverage means you pay a percentage and the parent/guardian pays the remainder. For example, "50% split" means you pay 50%, parent pays 50%. The exact split percentage is specified in the coverage rule.

---

**Q: Do LoGs automatically renew each year?**

A: No. New LoGs must be created for each school year. Admissions typically prepares new LoGs 2-3 months before the new school year starts. You'll be notified when ready for review.

---

**Q: Can I download all my LoGs at once?**

A: Currently, LoGs must be downloaded individually. However, you can generate a "My LoGs Report" that includes all LoG details in one document (though without full coverage rule details).

---

### Billing Questions

**Q: How do I get billed for covered expenses?**

A: ISM will bill you directly based on active LoGs. You'll receive invoices by email to your registered contact email. Payment instructions are included with each invoice.

---

**Q: What if I'm billed for something not covered in my LoG?**

A: Contact the Cashier office immediately: cashier@ism.edu.ph. Provide your Sponsor ID, invoice number, and LoG ID. Reference the specific coverage rule that indicates it's not covered.

---

**Q: Can I see billing history in the portal?**

A: The portal shows LoGs and coverage commitments, but detailed billing/invoices are handled separately by the Cashier's office. Contact them for billing statements.

---

**Q: When are payments due?**

A: Payment terms are specified on each invoice. Typically 30 days from invoice date. Late payment may affect student enrollment. Contact Cashier if you need payment arrangements.

---

### Technical Questions

**Q: Is my data secure?**

A: Yes. The system uses industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS), secure authentication, and role-based access control. Only you can see your sponsorship data. ISM staff access is logged and audited.

---

**Q: Can I access the portal from my mobile phone?**

A: Yes. The portal is mobile-responsive and works on smartphones and tablets. Some features may be easier on a desktop/laptop due to screen size.

---

**Q: Do I need to install any software?**

A: No. The portal is web-based and works in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). No special software or plugins required.

### Glossary

**Administrator:** ISM staff member with full system access who approves change requests and manages system settings.

**Admissions Staff:** ISM staff who create and manage sponsor records, students, and LoGs.

**Cashier:** ISM staff who handle billing and process payments.

**Change Request:** Formal submission requesting updates to your sponsor profile information, requiring Administrator approval.

**Coverage Rule:** Specific definition of what expenses you cover, including category, percentage/amount, and special conditions.

**Draft LoG:** Letter of Guarantee that has been created but not yet activated. Coverage is not in effect.

**LoG (Letter of Guarantee):** Formal document defining your coverage commitments for a specific student for a specific school year.

**Portal:** Self-service web application for sponsors to view their information and manage their sponsorships.

**School Year:** Academic year period (e.g., 2025-2026) running from August to May.

**Sponsor:** Organization or individual providing financial support for student(s). You are a sponsor.

**Sponsor ID:** Unique identifier for your organization in the system (e.g., SP-12345).

**Split Coverage:** Coverage arrangement where you pay a percentage and parent/guardian pays the remainder.

**Student ID:** Unique identifier for each student (e.g., STU-001234).

**TIN:** Tax Identification Number used for official documentation and tax reporting.
